The **Playground** is a live console inside Studio. Use it to test a channel rule, see what a payload looks like on the wire, or watch what a feature is publishing.

## Open it

**Services → Realtime → Playground** (under **Tools**).

## Watch a channel

Under **Watch a channel**, enter a channel name (for example `chat:lobby`, `orders:created`) and join. Until you do it says **Nothing watched yet.** The live view shows:

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **When** | Time of the message |
| **Event** | The event label |
| **User** | Who sent it |
| **Payload** | The data. Switch **Formatted** or **Raw** |

Presence members (**Present**) appear and disappear as people join and leave.

## Send a message

Enter an **event** name and a **payload** (JSON) and send. Everyone subscribed, in the Playground or in your app, receives it. Use it to simulate your server publishing: "send an `export.ready` to `user:42`".

## Useful checks

| Check | How |
| --- | --- |
| Is a rule too tight? | Join as the Playground (operator), then try the same channel from your app as a signed-in person |
| Is my payload right? | Send it here and look at **Raw** |
| Is the app publishing? | Watch the channel while you use the app |
| Do I receive table changes? | Watch `resource:tasks` while you edit a row |
| Does history work? | Rejoin and see the latecomer messages |

## Coming from other pages

Many Studio pages have a **Watch** link: from **Channels** and **Subscriptions** it opens the Playground on that channel.

## Limits

The Playground connects with Studio's own authority, so it ignores your Subscribe and Publish policies. To prove a rule, test from your app signed in as the person it should affect.
