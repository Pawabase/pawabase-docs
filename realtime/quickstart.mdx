You will make a `chat:*` family of channels where signed-in people can join and send, with the last 50 messages kept.

## 1. Create the channel rule in Studio

1. Open your project, choose `development`, then **Services → Realtime**.
2. In the sidebar choose **Channels** (under **Configure**).
3. Click **Add rule** (the page is titled **Channel rules**).
4. **Pattern:** `chat:*`.
5. **Kind:** *Presence* (tracks who is on the channel as well as messages).
6. **Subscribe policy:** *Signed-in users*. **Publish policy:** *Signed-in users*.
7. **Presence:** on. **History depth:** `50`.
8. Save. The rule appears in the list. New rules apply to new connections within about 30 seconds.

## 2. Try it in the Playground

1. Choose **Playground** (under **Tools**).
2. Enter `chat:lobby` as the channel and join it.
3. Send a message with the event `message` and a payload like `{"text": "hello"}`.

You see it arrive, formatted or raw (**Formatted / Raw**).

## 3. Do it from your app

Sign a person in ([Auth](/auth/overview)), then:

<CodeGroup>
```typescript TypeScript
const room = pawabase.realtime.channel("chat:lobby", { presence: { name: "Ada" } });

room.onEvent("message", (m) => addToScreen(m.from, m.payload.text));
room.on("presence", ({ members }) => showOnline(members));

await room.subscribe();
await room.send("message", { text: "hello from the app" });
```

```python Python
async with AsyncPawabase(url, key) as pb:
    room = pb.realtime.channel("chat:lobby", presence={"name": "Ada"})
    room.on("message", lambda m: print(m["payload"]["text"]))
    await room.subscribe()
    await room.send("message", {"text": "hello from the app"})
```
</CodeGroup>

The message you sent in the Playground appears in your app, and the reverse.

## 4. See it live in Studio

Go back to **Overview**: the channel shows under **Active channels**, your connection under **Connections**, and the counts of messages published and delivered climb.

## What you've learned

- Rules in Studio decide who can join and send.
- A pattern covers many channels (`chat:*`).
- The Playground lets you test without writing code.
