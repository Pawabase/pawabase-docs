## "subscribe() throws RealtimeError"

A rule refused you. In Studio open **Services → Realtime → Channels** and find the rule your channel name matches (rules are matched **top to bottom**, so an earlier broad pattern may win). Check **Subscribe policy** against who is signed in. If nothing matches, the **default rule** in **Settings** applies. New rules take up to about 30 seconds.

## "send() throws"

**Publish policy** refuses, or **Apps may publish** is off in **Settings**. For server-only channels, publish with a secret key.

## "I joined but receive nothing"

1. Is the sender publishing to the **exact** channel name and event?
2. Watch the channel in the **Playground**: do messages appear there?
3. Check **Overview → Published vs Delivered**.
4. Are you listening with `onEvent("name")` for the right event name? `on("message", …)` catches every message.

## "Messages arrive twice"

Normal after a reconnect with `replay`. The client removes duplicates by message id; if you render from your own state, merge by `id` too.

## "Presence is empty"

**Presence** is off for the channel's rule. Turn it on in **Channels**, and join with `{ presence: {...} }`.

## "History is empty"

**History depth** is 0 for the rule, or the channel is **Ephemeral**. Set a depth, or use **Durable** with a **Retention**.

## "Live records don't update"

- **Broadcast changes** must be on for the table (**Settings → Behaviour**).
- Your read rule must allow the row.
- You subscribed to `resource:<table>` exactly.
- If the change came from SQL, turn on **Capture changes made outside the API**.

## "Disconnects every few minutes"

A proxy closing idle connections. Check the heartbeat (default 25 seconds) is shorter than the proxy's timeout, or use [Server-Sent Events](/realtime/connection#one-way-streams). Watch **Connections** in Studio for churn.

## "Works locally, not in production"

Rules and channel patterns belong to an environment. Make sure the rule exists in `production`, and that the client uses the production key.

## "Too many connections"

Clients that never unsubscribe, or open a client per component. Create one client and reuse channels; disconnect on sign-out. A **Subscriber limit** on the rule caps a channel.

## "Rate limit hit"

**Publish rate limit** (per second) on the rule. Throttle noisy events on the client, or raise the limit.

## Collect this when asking for help

The channel name, the matching rule (screenshot **Channels**), who was signed in, the error code, and the connection id from **Connections**.
