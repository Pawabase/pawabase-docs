## See what you've used

Open **Usage & plan** in Studio to see what each limit allows and how much of it you've used this month. A project created without a plan has nothing capped, and usage is still counted.

## Environments

The free plan runs on the project's first environment, the one it was created with. Starter adds a second, usually production; Pro and above have room for staging and more. Studio shows how many your plan includes when you create one, and offers the upgrade when there's no room left.

If you move to a smaller plan while a project has more environments than the new plan includes, nothing is deleted: every environment keeps running, but you can't create another until you remove some or upgrade again.

## Monthly active users

A monthly active user is a distinct person who signed in or used your app in the month. People who never come back don't count.

## When you reach a limit

Studio shows it on **Usage & plan** before it bites. Upgrade from [Billing](/cloud/billing) when you're close. Plans are per organization, so an upgrade applies to all of its projects within the new limits.

Current prices are always on the [pricing page](https://pawabase.com/pricing).
