Plans and billing belong to the **organization**, not to a single project: every project in the organization shares its plan's limits. Open **Billing** in the dashboard. Only **owners and admins** can change billing.

## Choose a plan

1. **Billing → Upgrade** (or **Change plan**).
2. Pick a plan. Compare them on [Plans and limits](/cloud/plans-and-limits).
3. For a paid plan, you're sent to a secure payment page (run by our payment provider, Paystack), then brought back. The plan becomes active once the payment is confirmed.

Prices are shown in your currency for reference. The payment page shows exactly what you'll be charged.

Choosing the plan you're already on does nothing. A plan that's been retired can't be chosen.

## Move to the free plan

Choose **Free**. No payment is needed. If your organization has more projects than the free plan allows, you're told how many to delete first.

## Cancel

**Cancel** stops your plan **renewing**. You keep the plan until the end of the period you've paid for, then move to the free plan. The free plan has nothing to cancel.

## Update your card

**Update card** opens the payment provider's page for the card on your subscription.

## If a payment fails

The dashboard tells you the last payment failed and asks you to update your card to keep the plan. Update it under **Billing**.

## Promotion codes

A **promotion code** (like `PAWA-XXXX-XXXX`) gives an organization a paid plan **free for a number of days**. Enter it under **Billing → Have a promotion code?**

A code works when:

| Rule | If not, you're told |
| --- | --- |
| It exists and has started | *That code isn't valid* or *can be used from …* |
| It hasn't expired or been used up | *That code has expired* or *has already been used as many times as it allows* |
| The organization is on the **free** plan | *Codes are for organizations on the free plan* |
| The organization doesn't already have a promotion running | *already has a promotion until …* |
| The organization hasn't used this code before | *This organization has already used that code* |
| For codes meant for new customers: it's never had a paid plan or promotion | *That code is for organizations that have not had a paid plan or promotion before* |

When the promotion ends, the organization goes back to the free plan on its own unless you choose a paid plan. To stop guessing, too many wrong codes in a row make you wait an hour.

## Organization activity

The dashboard keeps a list of changes to projects and billing in the organization, newest first: who changed the plan, created or deleted a project, and when.

## Questions

For anything about a charge or a plan, contact support from the dashboard and include the organization's name.
