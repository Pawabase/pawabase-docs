Create buckets in Studio. A bucket is created once per environment and then used from your app by name.

## Where

**Services → Storage.** If you have no buckets the page says **No bucket yet**. Click **New bucket**.

## Steps

<Frame caption="Services → Storage → New bucket: Details and the Access section.">
  <img src="/images/studio/bucket-new.jpg" alt="Studio New bucket sheet" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name it">
    **Name:** lowercase letters, digits and dashes (for example `avatars`, `documents`, `exports`). It is unique within the environment and can't be changed later. `avatars` in `development` and in `production` are separate buckets with separate files.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose public or private">
    Turn **Public bucket** on if anyone with a file's address should be able to read it (avatars, product images). Leave it off for anything private. Uploads, listing and deletes always follow their own rules, whichever you choose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the five rules">
    **Who may read**, **Who may upload new objects**, **Who may list objects**, **Who may replace objects**, **Who may delete objects**. Each uses the [rule picker](/policies/rule-references). Start from the table below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Limit what can be uploaded">
    Under **Uploads**: **Accepted types** and **Largest file**. See [Bucket settings](/storage/bucket-settings).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create**. The bucket is ready at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common set-ups

| Bucket | Public | Read | Upload new | Replace / delete | Types, size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `avatars` | Yes | (public) | Signed-in, own folder | Own folder | `image/*`, 2 MB |
| `product-images` | Yes | (public) | Role `staff` | Role `staff` | `image/*`, 10 MB |
| `documents` | No | Own folder | Own folder | Own folder | PDF and office types, 25 MB |
| `team-files` | No | Same organization | Same organization | Same organization | any, 100 MB |
| `exports` | No | Signed-in | **Secret keys & Studio** | **Secret keys & Studio** | any |

"Own folder" is the custom rule `$object.segments.0` equals `$auth.user_id`; "same organization" compares `$object.segments.0` with `$auth.org`. See [Who can do what](/storage/access) and [Rules on storage](/policies/storage).

## Change a bucket later

Open the bucket and use its **settings**. You can change any setting except the name. A rule change applies to new requests within seconds.

## Delete a bucket

Deleting a bucket removes its configuration; the files should be removed first. In the file browser delete files and (empty) folders, then delete the bucket. Take a [backup](/backups/overview) if you may need them.

## How many buckets?

Use a separate bucket when the **rules** or **limits** differ: public vs private, images vs documents, user files vs server-written exports. Use folders (key prefixes) inside a bucket for everything else: per user, per organization, per year.
