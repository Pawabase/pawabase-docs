## Published public, drafts private

Readers see published posts; authors also see their drafts.

- **Any of** these rules pass:
  - **Compare values:** `$record.status` **equals** `published`
  - **Owns the record (field)** `author_id`

Use for `list` and `get`. (Per-row, so totals can be `null`; see [evaluation](/policies/how-rules-are-evaluated).)

## Hide deleted records

With a nullable `deleted_at` column ([soft delete](/database/patterns#soft-delete)):

- **All of** these rules pass:
  - **Owns the record (field)** `owner_id`
  - **Check a value:** `$record.deleted_at` **is empty**

## Scheduled publishing

Visible once `publish_at` has passed:

- **Any of** these rules pass:
  - **Check a value:** `$record.publish_at` **is empty**
  - **Compare values:** `$record.publish_at` **is at most** `$now`

A null `publish_at` means "publish immediately". Without the `empty` branch, those rows would never be visible (`lte` with a missing side is false).

## Expiring content

- **Any of** these rules pass:
  - **Check a value:** `$record.expires_at` **is empty**
  - **Compare values:** `$record.expires_at` **is greater than** `$now`

## Locked records

No edits once locked:

- **All of** these rules pass:
  - **Owns the record (field)** `owner_id`
  - **Compare values:** `$record.locked` **does not equal** yes

as `update` and `delete`. A rule can't *unlock*; do that from a service or a role: `{ "any": [{ "role": "admin" }, <the rule above>] }`.

## Only forward state changes

Let the owner move a record from `draft` to `submitted`, but not edit approved ones:

- **All of** these rules pass:
  - **Owns the record (field)** `owner_id`
  - **Compare values:** `$record.status` **is one of** `draft, submitted`
  - **Compare values:** `$input.status` **is one of** `draft, submitted`

as `update`. Approval is a separate step done by `role:reviewer` through another route.

## Limit values

- **All of** these rules pass:
  - **Is signed in**: yes
  - **Compare values:** `$input.quantity` **is at most** `10`
  - **Compare values:** `$input.quantity` **is at least** `1`

as `create`. For hard limits that must hold however the record is written, also set the column's `minimum`/`maximum` ([Columns](/database/columns-and-types)).

## Rate-limited by state

"At most 3 open orders" can't be a rule built in Studio: it needs a count of other rows. Do it in a [route](/logic/routes), or a unique partial [index](/database/indexes#partial-indexes), such as one open cart per customer.

## Honour the visibility of related records

A comment is visible if its post is. Comments can't look at the post in a rule built in Studio. Either copy the post's `visibility` onto the comment on create, or give comments the rule `public` and only expose them through the post with `expand`, which reads comments under the *comments* table's rule.
