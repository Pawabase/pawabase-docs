Some actions deserve extra protection: deleting data, exporting everything, changing payout details, administering users.

## Require a second factor

Choose **All** of: **Record owner**; **Signed in with 2FA** in the rule picker.

All must pass: the owner, signed in with a second step (`aal2`). A password alone is refused with a `403`.

### Step-up in your app

When a person is signed in at `aal1`, don't make them sign out. Catch the refusal, explain, and take them through the second step (the flow is in [Two-step verification](/auth/two-step-verification)), then retry:

```typescript
try {
  await pawabase.from("accounts").delete(id);
} catch (e) {
  if (e instanceof PermissionError) {
    show("For your security, confirm with your authenticator, then try again.");
  }
}
```

Check the level before you offer the action: `(await pawabase.auth.mfa.status()).aal` is `aal2` once the second step is done.

## Only your servers

Choose **Secret keys & Studio** in the rule picker.

Nothing from a browser can do it. Wrap it in a [route](/logic/routes) that checks everything and uses a secret key.

## Admin with a second factor and a company address

Pick **List** and add **Users with role** `admin`, **Signed in with 2FA**, and a **Custom condition** with *`$auth.email` ends with `@yourcompany.com`*. Also turn on **Require a verified email** in [Restrictions](/auth/sign-up-restrictions).

## Keys with a specific scope

For an integration key:

- **All of** these rules pass:
  - **Key has scope** `orders:export`
  - **Uses a secret key**

## Limit by caller kind

Allow people, not anonymous or service callers:

- **Caller kind is** `user`

## Allow from known addresses

- **Compare values:** `$request.ip` **starts with** `203.0.113.`

Crude, and addresses can be shared or change. Prefer the [firewall's](/firewall/overview) allow rules, or an API key's allowed addresses ([Keys](/policies/api-keys-and-scopes)).

## Make it hard to delete

- Leave `delete` off and use [soft delete](/database/patterns#soft-delete).
- Or *all of: Record owner; Signed in with 2FA*.
- Back up before bulk removals ([Backups](/backups/overview)).

## Audit it

Sensitive writes should leave a trail. Subscribe a flow to the table's events and record the `actor`, the record and the time in an `audit_log` table only `service` can write ([pattern](/database/patterns#audit-trail)). Also watch [sign-in activity](/auth/sign-in-activity).

## Alert on it

Use [Auth webhooks](/auth/auth-webhooks) for `user.mfa_disabled`, `user.disabled`, `session.reuse_detected`, and a webhook on your sensitive table's events to notify your team.
