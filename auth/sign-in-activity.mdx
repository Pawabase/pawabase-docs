Sign-in activity is the log of authentication events: sign-ins, sign-ups, resets and second-factor use, with success or failure.

## In Studio

**Users & auth → Records → Sign-in activity.** A table with columns: **When, User, Event, Method, Result, IP, Device**. Filter to failures to see attacks; open a person's **Activity** tab for just their history.

Use it for questions like:

- "Did Ada really sign in from there?" → open her **Activity**.
- "Is someone guessing passwords?" → filter **Result: failed**, look for one IP across many users.
- "Which methods do people use?" → group by **Method**.

## Show people their own activity

A "Recent sign-ins" list on the account page lets people spot unfamiliar activity:

```tsx
const events = await pawabase.auth.sessions.history();
events.map((e) => (
  <li key={e.at}>{new Date(e.at).toLocaleString()} — {e.method} — {e.success ? "ok" : `failed (${e.reason})`}</li>
));
```

## A weekly security routine

1. Review failed events for repeated failures against one account or from one address.
2. List users with the **MFA off** filter who hold admin roles and ask them to enrol.
3. Check [locked accounts](/auth/attack-protection).
4. Review [user API keys](/auth/user-api-keys) and revoke ones you don't recognise.
5. Take a backup: [import and export](/auth/import-and-export).
